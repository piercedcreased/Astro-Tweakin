# 🔥 Astro Tweakin v0.3.2: The Smelter

• The Shop's 🔥 **Smelter** button now opens its own menu. Pick exactly which bars to make: smelt all of one ore, make just 1 bar, or smelt everything at once.
• Each bar shows how much ore you have and how many bars you can afford to make.
• 🎲 Common ores now show their real odds. Stone was showing "1 in 1" but is really about 1 in 2, and Coal is really about 1 in 4. Drop rates didn't change, just the numbers you see.
