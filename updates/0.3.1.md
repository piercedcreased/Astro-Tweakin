# ⚡ Astro Tweakin v0.3.1: Locks, Smelt Picker and Speed

**New**
• 🔒 **Lock items** from the menu in your Inventory, or with **/lock**. Locked items are never sold (not even by Sell everything or auto-sell), Smelt all skips them, and Tweaker Tim won't fence them.
• 🔥 **Pick which ores to smelt** from the new menu in the Shop. Smelt all still works and skips locked ores.

**Faster**
• ⛏️ Mining and other buttons respond faster. Each mine click now saves once instead of 8 times.
• 🖼️ Common finds don't re-upload a picture every swing anymore. Rare and better finds still get one.
• 🎆 Rare-find animations are made in the background, so your mine doesn't freeze for a couple of seconds after a big find. Finding the same ore again reuses the animation.
