# 🛠️ Astro Tweakin v0.6.0: Fixes and a tidier mine

**/mine got reorganised**
• The main panel now has just Mine, Lucky Roll and Collect up top, then 🕳️ Mines, 🎒 Inventory, 🛒 Shop, 👤 Player and ⚙️ Settings.
• 🕳️ **Mines** replaces Up/Down: pick any layer you've unlocked from a list, or unlock the next one.
• 💰 **Selling only lives in your Inventory now**, so no more accidental sells next to the Mine button. There's a new picker to sell exactly the ores you choose, and "Sell everything" asks first.
• 🍬 Snacks and 🚀 Ship moved into the Inventory. 📜 Bounties, 🏆 Achievements and 📊 Leaderboard moved into 👤 Player.
• When a bounty is ready to claim, your mine panel tells you, and the Player button lights up.
• Your mine now shows a picture of the planet you're on, with your layer lit up and a depth gauge.
• ⌨️ Chat commands: `;mine` `;inv` `;shop` `;mines` `;player` `;bal` `;daily` `;lb` `;bounties` `;snacks` `;ship` `;help`

**Lucky Roll**
• ⚡ Haste is gone. Discord can't take button presses fast enough for it to matter.
• New prizes: 💰 **Midas Touch** (x1.5 sell value), 🧬 **Mutation Magnet** (x3 mutation chance) and the rarer 🔁 **Echo Swing** (every Mine press counts twice).
• The reel spins longer and stays in your panel, so it actually shows up even on a slow connection.

**Space Snacks**
• Snacks are much cheaper when you're starting out, but weaker. They get stronger the deeper you go and the better your pickaxe is.
• 🍬 Haste Gummies became **Mutation Mints**. If you had any, they've been swapped over.

**Fixes**
• The 📊 rarest-find leaderboard and your profile now count mutations, so a Galactic Coal can beat a plain Diamond.
• Meteor strikes no longer show the GIF twice.
• Rare-find GIFs now show your name properly, even with fancy fonts in your display name.
• Fixed the bot freezing for a moment during its automatic backups, which was one cause of the random "interaction failed" errors. Slow button presses now get answered in time instead of failing.
• Panel buttons now respect the server's game-channel setting, just like the slash commands.
