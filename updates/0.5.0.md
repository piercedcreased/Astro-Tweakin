# ☄️ Astro Tweakin v0.5.0: Asteroid Run

A new solo minigame for when nobody else is around. Open **🚀 Ship** and hit **☄️ Asteroid Run**.

• Pay some fuel and fly your rocket through a falling asteroid field with the ⬅️ ⬆️ ➡️ buttons.
• Every press moves you one lane and every rock falls one row, so there's no rush. Look ahead and plan your path.
• ✨ Fly through glowing ore chunks to grab them. They roll with double luck.
• 🏁 Survive 20 moves to dock at Tim's station: keep all your ore **plus 3x your fuel back in coins**.
• 💥 Hit a rock and you only keep half your ore. 🏳️ Or bail out any time and keep what you've got.
• One run every 10 minutes. Your rocket wears your ship paint.
