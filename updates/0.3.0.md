# 🚔 Astro Tweakin v0.3.0: Smuggler's Run

**The Space Authority is watching**
• Carry Legendary-or-rarer ore, and especially contraband, and you might get pulled over for a 🚔 **cargo check**.
• Pick your move before the cop loses patience: 🫣 hide your stash, 🗣️ smooth talk the officer (read his mood), 🐌 distract Sniffles the K-9 Slug with the right snack, 💸 bribe him, or 🗑️ jettison the evidence.
• Get busted and you pay a fine and lose your cheapest hot ore. The whole server hears about it.

**Your ship**
• 🚀 **/ship** shows your actual ship. Upgrade 🗄️ Hidden Compartments (fewer cargo checks), 🛡️ Reinforced Hull and 🔥 Turbo Engine, and give it a paint job.

**PvP (nobody loses their stash)**
• 🏴‍☠️ **/raid @someone** sneaks onto their ship and rolls for stuff they "forgot" about. They lose nothing. It's purely your luck vs their hull.
• ⚔️ **/duel @someone** for a best-of-3 laser fight, with an optional coin bet you both agree to.

**Events**
• ☄️ **Meteor strikes** hit once a day at a random time, and sometimes a bonus one sneaks in. First 5 to mine it get 8 rolls at x10 luck, and everyone gets x2 luck for 15 minutes.
• 👽 **Tweaker Tim** shows up a few times a day with sketchy deals: mystery boxes, cheap snacks, a weird rock that *might* be contraband, and he'll fence your hot ore for x1.5.

**More stuff to do**
• 🍬 **Space Snacks**: cook Luck Juice, Haste Gummies, Double Dip Chips, Cop Repellent and Mega Munchies.
• 📜 **Bounty board**: three new jobs every day, plus a bonus snack for finishing all three.
• 🏆 **29 achievements**, and every one is a title you can wear with **/title**.
• New buttons on your mine: Ship, Bounties, Snacks, Achievements.
