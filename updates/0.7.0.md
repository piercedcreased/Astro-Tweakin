# 💎 Astro Tweakin v0.7.0: Ultra Super Mega Crack Rock Edition

**✨ Pickaxe enchanting** (Shop → Enchant)
• Every pickaxe has 3 enchant slots. Enchanting a slot gives a random enchant: 🍀 Fortune, 🧬 Mutagen, 💰 Midas, 🔁 Echo, 💎 Prospector or 🐌 Slug Whisperer, at level I, II or III.
• Don't like what you got? Each slot gets **one** reroll for 10,000 coins, right after you enchant it.
• After that it's locked in, unless you find a 💎 **Enchanted Crack Rock** (1 in 2,500 per Mine). A Crack Rock empties the slots you pick: one, two or all three. It's used up when you press Done.
• Enchants don't carry over when you craft your next pickaxe.

**🌦️ Planet weather**
• Every planet gets new weather every 3 hours: ☀️ Solar Flare, 🌪️ Dust Storm, ☄️ Meteor Season, 🌌 Aurora, 💰 Gold Rush, 🔮 Crystal Fog and the rare 🌀 Tweaker Weather. Each one changes your luck, mutations, sell prices or Crack Rock odds.
• Your mine shows the current weather. `/weather` shows every planet.

**📦 Crates**
• 📦 Supply, 🌌 Cosmic and 🌀 Tweaked Crates spin open right in your panel. Inside: coins, snacks, ore hauls, mutated ores, Lucky Roll recharges, Crack Rocks and even better crates.
• Get them from minigames, bounties and the daily calendar, or buy them (Inventory → Crates, or `/crates`).

**📅 Daily calendar**
• `/daily` is now a 7-day calendar. Day 7 is a 🌌 Cosmic Crate. Miss a day and you only drop back one step.

**🐌 Slug Den** (Shop → Slug Den)
• Prestige 10 sluglings into a better kind of slug: Cave, Crystal, Magma, Cosmic and then Tweaked Slugs. Each kind digs faster and luckier.
• 🎒 Bigger Backpacks let your sluglings dig for up to 24h while you're away.

**🎲 Mining minigames**
• Every so often while mining, a quick minigame pops up in your panel: pick the rock, help Tim count, or grab the right gem. You always get coins, and a right answer also gets a Supply Crate. It shows up at least once every 250 Mines.

**🃏 Space Jack** (🎮 Arcade on your mine panel, or `/arcade`)
• Blackjack against Tweaker Tim. Hit, stand or double down. Blackjack pays 3:2.

**📜 Weekly and monthly bounties**
• The bounty board now has Daily, Weekly and Monthly pages. Clear the weekly board for a Cosmic Crate and the monthly one for a Tweaked Crate. Clearing the daily board now also gives a Supply Crate.

**⌨️ Every command works with ;**
• `;raid @player`, `;duel @player 500`, `;profile`, `;crates`, `;enchant`, `;arcade`, `;weather`, `;travel 3` and the rest.

**Fixes**
• Asteroid Run: a button press made while the last move was still loading used to count against the new position, so it could look like a rock beside you hit you. Presses on an older picture are now ignored, and a crash tells you whether a rock fell onto you or you steered into one.
