# 🛠️ Astro Tweakin v0.2.0: Quality of Life

**New**
• ♻️ **Auto-sell** button on your mine. Turn it on and common and uncommon ores sell the moment you dig them up. Rare stuff and anything you need for your next pickaxe or planet is kept.
• 🎯 **Goal tracker** on your mine shows progress bars toward your next pickaxe and your next planet.
• 📅 **/daily** gives free coins every day. Keep a streak for up to +100%, and every 7th day you also get a free Lucky Roll and 30 minutes of x2 luck.
• 🔎 **/ore** looks up any ore: its picture, odds, value and where to find it. Ores you haven't found stay secret.
• 💎 **/finds** shows the latest 1-in-10,000+ finds across the server.
• 🛒 The shop has a brand new look.

**Changes**
• ⛏️ Mining is way faster. Cooldowns now start at 1 second and drop to 0.3s with the best pickaxe.
• No more "catching your breath" spam when you click too fast.
