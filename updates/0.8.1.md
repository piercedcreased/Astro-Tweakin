# 🔧 Astro Tweakin v0.8.1: Fixes and polish

**🃏 Space Jack**
• Hands are now drawn on a card table with real-looking cards and big totals for you and Tim. Tim's face-down card shows as "10+?" until he plays, and a WIN / BUST / PUSH / BLACKJACK banner shows at the end.
• One 🃏 Space Jack button replaces the three fixed bets: type any bet you want (100 minimum, `5k` and `1.5m` work). After a hand you get "Deal again" at the same bet or "✏️ Change bet".

**☄️ Asteroid Run**
• If you steer toward a rock that's right beside you, you now hop up and over it instead of crashing. Otherwise left and right work like before.

**🪨 Rock Paper Scissors**
• After a game, either player can press 🔁 **Rematch** to play again at the same bet (for 10 minutes).

**🐌 Sluglings**
• The Collect button shows how many ores are waiting, and it turns green with "full!" once your sluglings have stopped digging.
• Fixed: pressing Collect too early or partway through a minute threw away that digging time. It now carries over.

**Panels**
• 📅 When your daily calendar day is ready, the 👤 Player button counts it and your mine panel tells you.
• The 🎯 next pickaxe and depth goals now only show in the 🛒 Shop, not on the main mine page.
• 🏪 Market now sits before ↩️ Back in the Shop and Inventory.
