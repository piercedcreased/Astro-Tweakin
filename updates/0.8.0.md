# 🤝 Astro Tweakin v0.8.0: Multiplayer Update

**🪨📄✂️ Rock Paper Scissors** (`/rps @player [bet]` or `;rps @player 500`)
• Challenge anyone, with or without a coin bet. Both of your profile pictures go side by side with a ❓ for each of you.
• Pick in secret. Your side flips to ❗ once you've chosen, but nobody can see what you picked. When the second player picks, both choices are revealed and the winner gets crowned (and takes the bet).
• Ties replay automatically. Either player can back out before it ends, and then no coins move.

**🏪 Astro Market** (`/market`, or the 🏪 Market button in Shop, Inventory and Player)
• A player shop like Cosmic Prisons' auction house. List ores, mutations, bars, crates, Crack Rocks, snacks and tags for any price you want.
• Your items stay safe in the Market until they sell. When something sells, the coins land in your balance automatically (minus a 5% fee), and you get a DM.
• Browse by category: Ores, Mutations, Tags or Other. Flip pages, then buy with the dropdown.
• Anything that hasn't sold after 48h comes back to you. 📋 My listings lets you cancel a listing early and shows your recent sales. You can have up to 8 things listed at once.

**🏷️ Tags** (Inventory → 🏷️ Tags, or `/tags`)
• 🏷️ **Pickaxe Rename Tag**: give your pickaxe its own name. It shows on your mine and profile, and the name stays when you upgrade.
• 🔄 **Enchant Reset Tag**: every enchant slot that already used its reroll gets that reroll back.
• 📛 **Custom Title Tag**: write your own title instead of wearing an achievement.
• You can get tags from 🌌 Cosmic and 🌀 Tweaked Crates, or buy one from Tweaker Tim's new 🏷️ Shady Tag deal. You can also trade them on the Market.

**💸 /pay @player amount** sends coins to a friend (`;pay @player 5k` works too).

**🏆 New achievements**: ✊ Rock Solid (win 10 games of RPS) and 🏪 Space Merchant (sell 10 things on the Market).
