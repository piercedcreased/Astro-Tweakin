# 📊 Astro Tweakin v0.3.4: Leaderboards and Fixes

• 📊 **Leaderboards** got a big upgrade. There's a new Leaderboard button on your mine, and **/leaderboard** now ranks coins, rarest find, ores mined, furthest planet, index, achievements, raids, duels, bounties and... times busted. If you're not in the top 10 it shows your rank.
• 🖼️ Every ore shows its picture again when you mine. The pictures are smaller files now, so mining stays fast.
• 🛠️ Behind the scenes: if something breaks, the bot now sends a report straight to the developer so it gets fixed faster.
