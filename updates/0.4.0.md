# 🧬 Astro Tweakin v0.4.0: Mutations

Every ore in the game can now come out of the ground **mutated**. Same ore, way more coins:

• ✨ **Shiny** (1 in 100) · worth x3
• ☢️ **Radioactive** (1 in 400) · worth x6, glows like it shouldn't
• 🌑 **Shadow** (1 in 1,000) · worth x10, has eyes. Gets a reveal animation.
• 🌈 **Holographic** (1 in 2,500) · worth x20, rainbow foil. Announced to the server.
• 🌌 **Galactic** (1 in 10,000) · worth x50, there's a whole galaxy in it
• 🌀 **Tweaked** (1 in 50,000) · worth x150, completely cooked

A Tweaked Coal is still worth 150 coal. A Tweaked contraband is worth retiring over.

• 🧬 Mutated ores have their own section in your inventory and their own picture. Regular **Sell** keeps them; only **Sell everything** sells them. You can 🔒 lock them, and they come with you when you rebirth.
• 🐌 Sluglings, raids, meteors and Tweaker Tim's boxes can all turn up mutations too.
• 🏆 Five new achievements (and titles): Shiny Hunter, Holo Collector, Galaxy Brain, Fully Tweaked and Mutant Menagerie (one of every mutation).
• 📊 New **Mutations found** leaderboard, and your mutation counts are on **/profile**.
